# NHANES Diabetes Analysis

Status: complete. The analysis is running and the full written report is being finished.

## Research question
How are BMI, physical activity, age, and blood pressure associated with diabetes among U.S. adults?

## Data
National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES), 2017 to March 2020 cycle, from the CDC's National Center for Health Statistics. Public, de-identified data.

## Methods
Python (pandas, scipy, statsmodels, matplotlib, seaborn) in a Google Colab notebook. Group comparisons with t-tests and a chi-square test, then a multivariable logistic regression.

## Why I did this
I was curious about how different factors are associated with diabetes, so I decided to explore CDC NHANES data on my own. The project gave me a glimpse of how I could use statistics to study real-world health problems in the future and strengthened my interest in pursuing biostatistics.

## Note
This is an observational, cross-sectional analysis, so it shows associations, not causation.
